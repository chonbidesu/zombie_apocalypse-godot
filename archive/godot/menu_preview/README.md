# Character room art notes

These notes document the room's artwork and animation history. Selected textures moved to `godot/assets/character_creation/` when the room joined the main game.

The room uses the ivy-free background and registered character atlases in `layers/`. Targeted variants for the private, medic, scout and body bag live in `card_room/`. The medic and scout share a table. The scout and her chair draw behind the medic's tabletop and backpack. The scout reveals her hand when confirmed. The medic lays her hand face up on the table.

Hover a group, click a character to lock the spotlight, then Confirm. Cancel, right-click and Escape clear selection. Confirmation uses a direct camera approach and fades the room and other characters.

Hover plays four drawn idle frames. Some characters use a mask to hold stationary furniture on its resting drawing; the consumer and police officer render complete frames directly. The body bag has its own kick, shove and rest sequence. Its stretcher uses uniform scaling. Floor shadows project the current silhouette away from each overhead light and meet the sprite at its supports. These are floor-only shadows. Reduce motion freezes idle playback and camera movement.

Run `godot --path godot --script res://tests/character_creation_test.gd -- --motion-review` to capture all nine sets of poses against the room.

The firefighter uses `layers/firefighter-size-matched.png`: a 1448×1086 transparent atlas with twelve 362×362 cells. The four idle drawings have a shorter, slightly narrower torso and matching leg proportions to prevent a size change at confirmation. They retain the axe-on-shoulder breathing poses and 0–1–2–3–2–1 playback. The eight confirmation drawings are copied pixel-for-pixel from `firefighter-axe-lift.png`: lift the axe clear of her shoulder, carry it forward while raised, lower it toward her open hand, close her glove around the handle, and settle into a two-handed ready stance. All poses use her healthy portrait's face, brown hair and black helmet. Playback shows complete frames without interpolation or procedural deformation; timing is unchanged. `firefighter-idle-size-prompt.txt` records the size correction and `firefighter-axe-lift-prompt.txt` records the confirmation redraw. Preview checks verify the confirmation rows remain pixel-identical.

The doctor's face and dark hair come from `layers/doctor-portrait.png`, drawn to match her healthy portrait in all twelve existing poses. A fixed head/neck cutout in `idle.gdshader` uses the new drawing while retaining the original `doctor-refined.png` for the body, clothes, hands and jukebox. The original frame timing and movement remain intact. `doctor-portrait-prompt.txt` records the built-in imagegen prompt.

The consumer and police officer use `layers/consumer-full-redraw.png` and `layers/police-full-redraw.png`. These are complete replacement 4×3 atlases, each containing four idle and eight confirmation drawings. They render directly with no shader, patches, detail overlays, or held furniture regions. The consumer retains the leaning pool-shot sequence with a newly drawn front bridge hand; the officer retains the seated cleaning, cloth placement, slide and aiming sequence. Original playback timing remains unchanged. `full-sequence-redraw-prompts.txt` records the built-in imagegen prompts. The preview checks also verify that every frame uses the replacement atlas and neither actor has a compositing material.

The consumer's latest redraw starts from the original smaller leaning figure and shallow, oblique table with three visible legs. Her small left open bridge rests near the far rail on her side, supporting the cue, with a separate cue ball and more clear felt before the centered rack. She sights along the shot; the portrait serves as appearance inspiration. Four idle drawings include subtle breathing, followed by eight backswing, strike and ball-scatter drawings. Placement, cinematic focus, floor contacts and playback timing are unchanged. `consumer-original-scale-redraw-prompt.txt` records the base redraw; `consumer-finger-definition-prompt.txt` records the subsequent refinement of all twelve bridge hands with clearly separated, tapered fingers and a distinct side thumb.
